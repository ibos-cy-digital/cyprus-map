02Applications

For spaces where people live and work.

Electrical work is needed in many kinds of spaces. Each has its own requirements, and we adapt our approach to them.

  • Residential buildings

    Flats and houses: new installations, extensions, lighting and replacing outdated equipment, with respect for the space you live in.

  • Commercial spaces

    Offices, shops and workspaces that need a reliable installation, a well-organised distribution board and good lighting.

  • Adaptations & renovations

    Adapting the installation to a new layout — ideally during renovation, before finishing work begins.

03Approach

Precision in every detail.

BR ELECTRIC is an electrical services provider based in Niš. A good installation starts before the first cable — with understanding the space, the needs and the expectations.

That is why we approach every job systematically: from agreement, through execution, to checking that everything works as it should.

  1. 01

    Careful planning

    Before work begins, we assess the space and agree on a solution that fits your actual needs.

  2. 02

    Professional execution

    Tidy work, clearly routed wiring and attention to the details that end up out of sight.

  3. 03

    Clear communication

    Throughout the job you know what is being done, why, and in what order.

  4. 04

    Functional, reliable solutions

    The goal is an installation that works safely and logically in everyday use.
